text-align, text-decoration & line-height

Three properties that shape how a text block reads: alignment on the page, decoration like underlines, and the vertical space between lines.

Live Demo

text-align: center

text-decoration: underline

line-height: 1.8 — this line has extra space above and below it so longer paragraphs stay easy to read even at a glance.

Code

HTML
<p style="text-align: center;">Centered text</p>
<p style="text-decoration: underline;">Underlined text</p>
<p style="line-height: 1.8;">Loosely spaced paragraph text…</p>
Inline Style
text-align: center;
text-decoration: underline;
line-height: 1.8;
